## Plot

Same kind of meadow as the accepted smooth-ribbon model.

- One low sand mound. The sand holds every shoot.
- One rhizome with a few branches, so the shoots sit across the sand.
- 12–16 shoots. Younger shoots are shorter.
- Fine roots leave each node, spread across the sand, then go down.
- 30 fps, frames 1–330, one `Grow` clip. Frame 330 matches frame 1. Frame 300 is the full patch.
- Under 40,000 triangles. No camera, light, curve, or reef node in the `.glb`.

## This plant

Facts are only the `cymodocea_serrulata` record.

- Flat strap, about 5–9 mm wide. Use 9 mm so it reads wider than the smooth-ribbon display.
- Serrated tip. Do not write a tooth count.
- Sheath broadly triangular.
- Leaf scars do not meet around the stem.
- The patch spreads when the buried rhizome grows and sends up shoots. Roots grow down at each node.
- No flowers in the mesh.

A front view shows the wide strap and the serrated tip. A side view shows the thin edge. The stem shows an open scar line, not a closed ring.
