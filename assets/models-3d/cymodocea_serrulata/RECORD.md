# Cymodocea serrulata

Built in the Blender window that was already open. The phone model and the lesson are the same meadow: wide straps with serrated tips, a broadly triangular sheath, leaf scars that do not meet, a pale buried stem, and fine roots at the nodes. The sand is a solid uneven bed with a front cut.

| Role | File |
|------|------|
| Blender for the phone model | `cymodocea_serrulata.blend` |
| Blender for the lesson | `cymodocea_serrulata_lesson.blend` |
| Phone model | `cymodocea_serrulata.glb` — 36,146 triangles, one Grow clip, no camera |
| Lesson video | `cymodocea_serrulata_lesson.mp4` — 30.000 seconds, 1920×1080, speech and bottom subtitles, no music |

The same two files are in `app/assets/models-3d/`. The voice is Microsoft David Desktop, already on this PC. The spoken words are the `transcript` for this slug in `app/assets/data/species.json`.
