## Not a short ribbon

The longest leaves, edges rolled in, a thick rhizome with black bristles, and cord-like roots. About four shoots so it stays under 40,000 triangles. A short strap meadow fails this plan.

Presentation: a low view up the long blades, then the cut face of the bed where the bristled rhizome and cord roots are visible. Do not use the ribbon-tip lesson camera.

Roots: cord-like, not the fine threads used on the other species. The rhizome is thick and carries long black bristles.

Seabed: the shared uneven bed. The plant is larger, so more of the thick rhizome may sit in the sand. Do not model a mangrove and do not build a private flat sand.
