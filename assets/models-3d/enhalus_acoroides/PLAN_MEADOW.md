- One thick rhizome with a few branches, so the shoots sit across the sand.

## This plant

Facts are only the `enhalus_acoroides` record.

- Very long ribbon leaves, about 30–150 cm. Pick a display length inside that range and comment it.
- Margins roll inward.
- Rhizome is thick, with long black bristles.
- Roots are cord-like.
- The meadow spreads by that thick buried rhizome.
- Male and female flowers are on separate plants. This model does not show the flowers.

A front view shows the long ribbons and the rolled edges. The rhizome reads thick, with black bristles, and the roots read as cords.
