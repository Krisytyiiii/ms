# Enhalus acoroides

One species. A display of tape seagrass for the phone and the lesson. Out of scope: a mangrove, a flower mesh, a growth clip, pitch audio, and any file under `cymodocea_rotundata/`.

## Solid geometry

The phone file is a solid meadow: a thick rhizome, four shoots, ribbon leaves, cord-like roots, and long black bristles on the rhizome. No flower. No mangrove.

## Close to the record

The record in `docs/21-3d-species-model-spec.md` says very long ribbon leaves, inrolled margins, a thick rhizome with long black bristles, and cord-like roots. The patch spreads by that buried stem. It can flower and set seed; the flower is spoken and not meshed. Sources for the profile line are in `profile_update.json`.

## Display choices

Leaf length stays inside the record's range. Leaf width, bristle length, and bristle count are display choices in `enhalus_acoroides_build/config.py`. They are not spoken.

## Phone file

`enhalus_acoroides.blend` and `enhalus_acoroides.glb`. Four shoots. Action `Grow`, frames 1–330 at 30 fps. Frame 330 matches frame 1. No camera. 21,908 triangles.

## Lesson

`enhalus_acoroides_lesson.blend` and `enhalus_acoroides_lesson.mp4`. Thirty seconds, 1920×1080, 30 fps, frames 1–900. English speech with matching bottom subtitles, no music. The lesson blend is not the phone file.
