# Halodule uninervis

Needle seagrass for the phone viewer and the 30-second lesson. The meadow is a display, not a scan of a Mati meadow.

Each leaf has one central vein and a trident tip. The rhizome is pale ivory, with clean black leaf scars at the nodes. Leaf width and leaf length are display choices in `halodule_uninervis_build/config.py`. They are not spoken.

The phone file plays `Grow` from frame 1 to frame 330 at 30 fps, and frame 330 matches frame 1. The lesson is 30 seconds at 1920×1080 with the five spoken lines and no music.
