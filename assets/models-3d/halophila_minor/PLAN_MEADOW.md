## Plot

Same kind of meadow as the accepted smooth-ribbon model.

- One low sand mound. The sand holds every shoot.
- One rhizome with a few branches, so the pairs sit across the sand.
- 12–16 nodes. Each node carries one pair of leaves. There is no upright fan.
- Fine roots leave each node, spread across the sand, then go down.
- 30 fps, frames 1–330, one `Grow` clip. Frame 330 matches frame 1. Frame 300 is the full patch.
- Under 40,000 triangles. No camera, light, curve, or reef node in the `.glb`.
- New builder: `assets/models-3d/minor_next/`. Draft blend and `.glb` under `assets/models-3d/halophila_minor_next/`. Never under `app/`.
- After the checks pass, move the current `app/assets/models-3d/halophila_minor.glb` into this folder’s `earlier/`, then copy the new file onto that app path.

Every layout number that is not in the record is a display choice and is commented as one.

## This plant

Facts are only the `halophila_minor` record.

- Small oval leaves in pairs, straight from the buried rhizome.
- Smooth margins.
- Wedge-shaped leaf sheath.
- Fewer than eight pairs of cross veins. Use a display count inside that limit and comment it.
- The record does not give a millimetre size. Keep the leaf small enough that the ovalis display will read larger, and comment the size as a display choice.
- The patch spreads when the buried rhizome grows. Roots grow down at the nodes.
- No flowers in the mesh.
