# Halophila minor

Small spoon seagrass. One phone patch and one 30-second lesson.

The plant is paired oval leaves on petioles from a buried stem. The margin is smooth. The sheath is wedge-shaped. There is no erect strap fan. Cross-vein pairs are a display choice on `HM_Controls`, inside the Seagrass-Watch range of fewer than eight. Eight or more would be spoon seagrass.

Leaf length, leaf width, and petiole length are display choices in the builder config. They are not a Mati measurement.

The lesson speaks the five lines in `profile_update.json`. There is no growth clip and no pitch audio.
