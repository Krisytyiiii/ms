# Plan — *Syringodium isoetifolium* only

An agent that builds this species reads this file and no other species plan.

Slug: `syringodium_isoetifolium`. Prefix: `SI`. Common name: noodle seagrass.

Private builders: `assets/models-3d/syringodium_isoetifolium_build/`. Blender: `C:\Program Files\Blender Foundation\Blender 5.2\blender.exe` in the background.

## Not a strap

Every other strap species is flat. This one is round in every view. A flattened leaf fails even if the script says “spaghetti”.

Presentation: the lesson turns so the leaf is seen from the side and from the end. The point at the tip is visible. Do not use the ribbon-tip camera.

Roots: fine roots at the nodes on the buried stem. The record does not describe bristles or cord-like roots, so do not add them.

Seabed: the shared uneven bed only.

## What a person must see

This is the only species whose leaf is round from every side.

- Cylindrical leaf, like spaghetti. A flat strap fails this model.
- Tip tapers to a point.
- Length stays inside the record’s 7–30 cm. Diameter and sheath shape are OPEN. Do not speak a diameter or a sheath shape as a fact.

## Lesson

30 seconds, 1920×1080. No floating 3D text plates. Subtitles along the bottom. No music.

Voice: pause, then ask the viewer to turn it. Slow down on “round”.

Spoken script, also this slug’s `transcript` only:

This is noodle seagrass, Syringodium isoetifolium. A display, not a scan of a meadow in Mati.

Turn the plant. The leaf stays round. It is not a flat strap. The tip tapers to a point, the way a piece of spaghetti does.

The leaves are about 7 to 30 centimetres long. The patch spreads when the buried stem grows through the sand and sends up new shoots. Roots grow down at each node. It can also flower and set seed.
