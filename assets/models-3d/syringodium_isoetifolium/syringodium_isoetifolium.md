# Syringodium isoetifolium

Noodle seagrass. A display, not a scan of a meadow in Mati.

The leaf stays round. It is not a flat strap. The tip tapers to a point, the way a piece of spaghetti does. Leaves are about 7 to 30 centimetres long. The buried stem grows through the sand and sends up new shoots. Roots grow down at each node. The plant can also flower and set seed.

Current files are named in `RECORD.md`. Sizes the record leaves open stay in the builder CONFIG.
