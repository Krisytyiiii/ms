## Plot

Same kind of meadow as the accepted smooth-ribbon model.

- One low sand mound. The sand holds every shoot.
- One rhizome with a few branches, so the shoots sit across the sand.
- 12–16 shoots. Younger shoots are shorter.
- Roots leave each node, spread across the sand, then go down. The record says they grow down at the nodes and does not call them cord-like.
- 30 fps, frames 1–330, one `Grow` clip. Frame 330 matches frame 1. Frame 300 is the full patch.
- Under 40,000 triangles. No camera, light, curve, or reef node in the `.glb`.

## This plant

Facts are only the `thalassia_hemprichii` record.

- Curved ribbon leaves, about 10–40 cm. Pick a display length inside that range and comment it.
- Short black bars of tannin cells in the blade. Bar count is not in the record. Comment the count as a display choice.
- Rhizome is thick, with scars between the shoots.
- The patch spreads when that thick buried rhizome grows and sends up shoots.
- No flowers in the mesh.
