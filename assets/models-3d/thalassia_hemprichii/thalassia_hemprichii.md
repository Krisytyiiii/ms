# Thalassia hemprichii

Sickle seagrass. A display, not a scan of a Mati meadow.

The buried stem grows through the sand. Roots grow down at each node. Each leaf is a curved ribbon with short black bars in the blade. The rhizome is thick, with scars between the shoots. It can flower and set seed.

Leaf length stays inside the record range. Leaf width and tannin-bar density are display choices on `TH_Controls` in `thalassia_hemprichii_build/config.py`. They are not spoken.

One species only. No growth clip. No pitch audio. No blend under `app/`.
